WHY TRUST US

Why should you trust us?

JANNE BAUTISTA

“ First time visiting and it was a great experience. The portions are huge and we ended up taking our leftovers to go. You also get unlimited rice and side dishes with the main meals, which is an awesome deal. The service was really nice too, when I asked for everything, including the appetizers, to come out at the same time, they made it happen with no problem. Seating is limited, so I’d recommend making a reservation. Overall, very satisfying! ”

CHARLIE LEE

“ Authentic Korean dishes. Have tried all items on the dishes in the past couple of years. Absolutely love every single dishes. The only gotchas the short ribs sometimes have much more fat than other time. I suppose it all depends on the source of the supplier. Otherwise it well cooked and prepared. Of course the space and seating are very limited. If you would have decided to go on the weekends or dinner time, reservations are highly recommended. ”

WC

“ I’ve been a customer of this restaurant for almost a year, and the food quality has always been excellent—whether dining in or ordering takeout. Dining in is definitely the better option, with free refills of rice and side dishes. I especially love the BBQ chicken, where the sweet and spicy flavours are perfectly balanced, and the pork belly is truly a hidden gem. For dinner parties at home, we always order the seafood pancake—the portion is generous and easily gives everyone at least a couple of bites. Highly recommended! ”
